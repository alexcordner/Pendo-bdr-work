# Who Pendo helps (edit me; plain English)

> Starter draft from your brief. Please rewrite in your own words. Anything below can be changed.

## Company profile
- Companies up to ~2,000 employees in Australia, New Zealand and wider APAC (mainly ANZ).
- They own a digital product people use: a customer-facing app or web platform (B2B or B2C),
  or internal software their employees must adopt (digital adoption).
- They have, or are building, a product / digital / CX / support team.

## Pain Pendo solves
- Don't know how people actually use the product (no analytics, or analytics nobody trusts).
- Low adoption or onboarding drop-off of new features or internal tools.
- Support tickets caused by confusing UX that in-app guidance could deflect.
- Feedback scattered across email, Slack and tickets.
- Big rollout in flight (new app, ERP/CRM/HRIS migration) and no way to guide users.

## Buyer personas
Product, digital, CX, support, IT / digital workplace, engineering leadership. See `config.yaml` for titles.

## Triggers that matter
New product or app launch, funding, new product/digital/CX/CTO leader, hiring product or CX roles,
digital transformation or platform migration, M&A integration.

## Triggers to ignore
Generic hiring (nurses, drivers, legal), earnings with no product angle, anything not tied to a buyer above.

## Poor fit
No digital product, pure services shops with no software, tiny teams without a product function,
anything already a Pendo customer (check Salesforce).
