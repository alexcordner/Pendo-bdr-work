# Email-draft skill (DRAFT v0: to be tuned in Phase 2/3)

Output is a Gmail **draft** only. Never send.

## Shape
- Subject: 2-5 words, lowercase-ish, specific, not clickbait (e.g. "tried you re: new app").
- Body: 3 to 5 short lines, under 80 words.
  1. "Just tried you." plus one clause saying why (the real trigger).
  2. One sentence on why that matters for someone in their role.
  3. One ask, a question or a 15-minute slot. Only one ask.
- Sign off: "Alex" and the standard signature (Gmail adds it).

## Rules
- Reference only a trigger that is in the call sheet record. No trigger, no pretend trigger: use an honest, role-based line instead.
- No fluff openers, no "I hope this finds you well", no attachments, no links except where Alex asks.
- No "quick", "touch base", "circle back", "synergy". No emojis. No exclamation marks.
- Don't claim Pendo customer names or stats unless supplied and verified in the input.
- Skip (log, don't draft) if: no email, do-not-contact/unsubscribed, or emailed in the last 7 days.
