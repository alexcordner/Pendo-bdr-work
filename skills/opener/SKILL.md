# Opener skill (DRAFT v0: to be tuned in Phase 2 until it sounds like Alex)

Calls mostly last under 10 seconds and only about 1 in 4 pass 30 seconds. The opener is the whole game.

## Output (exactly this shape, plain text)
```
OPENER: <one line, max ~25 words, said out loud in about 8 seconds>
FACT 1: <specific fact> (source: <where it came from + date>)
FACT 2: <specific fact> (source: <where it came from + date>)
GATEKEEPER: <one line asking for the right person by role, not a pitch>
TRIGGER: <real trigger, or "None found: cold call on ICP fit only">
```

## Rules
- Tie the opener to the real trigger if there is one. If there is none, say so in TRIGGER and write an honest fit-based opener. Never invent a trigger, a quote or a fact.
- Facts must come from data in the sheet input (scoop, Salesforce, Nooks, ZoomInfo, website text). Cite source and date. If you only have one real fact, give one and write "FACT 2: none verified".
- Do not mention Pendo features in the opener. One idea: relevance to their situation, then a question.
- Permission-based or curiosity opener. No "Hope you're well", no "I'm reaching out", no "quick call".
- Plain Australian business English. Short sentences. No hype words (leverage, synergy, game-changing, revolutionise).
- Gatekeeper line: ask for the role ("the person who looks after your app / product team"), keep it under 15 words, no pitch.
