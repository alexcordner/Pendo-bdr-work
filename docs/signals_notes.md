| exec_move | 0.90 | role ambiguous x0.7 | 45 |
| product_launch | 0.80 | physical or unclear launch: 0.40 (x0.5); fund / property / financial product launch: 0.20 (x0.25) | 21 |
| project_pain | 0.80 | | 21 |
| new_hire | 0.65 | role ambiguous x0.7 | 45 |
| funding | 0.60 (was 0.70) | debt facility / loan / bond / credit line: 0.25 | 21 |
| hiring_plan | 0.55 | | 21 |
| ma | 0.50 (was 0.60) | target 0.50; acquirer of a digital business or a full acquisition 0.50; acquirer of a minority stake or unrelated business 0.25; direction unclear 0.25; acquirer in reported talks x0.5 of the acquirer weight | 21 |
| partnership | 0.30 (was 0.45) | without product / digital / customer words: 0.20 | 21 |
| intent (UNVERIFIED) | 0.30, cap 0.30 | | 21 |

`strength = round(round(base * 0.5 ** (age_days / half_life), 4) * variant factor [* 0.7 if role ambiguous], 4)`. Constants: `KIND_BASE_WEIGHT`, `LAUNCH_NON_DIGITAL_WEIGHT` (0.40), `MA_MINORITY_OR_UNRELATED_WEIGHT` / `MA_UNCLEAR_WEIGHT` (0.25), `PARTNERSHIP_NO_PRODUCT_WEIGHT` (0.20), `RECENCY_HALF_LIFE_DAYS`, `KIND_HALF_LIFE_DAYS`. There are no new `SignalKind` values (the enum is shared), so variants are multipliers carried on the verdict (`ScoopVerdict.factor`, `.notes`) and shown on the signal as evidence notes.
Literal checks: exec_move 21 days 0.6513, 45 days 0.45; funding 45 days 0.1359; digital launch 5 days 0.6783, physical launch 0.3392; M&A target 5 days 0.4239, minority acquirer 0.2119; partnership 5 days 0.2544 / 0.1696 without product words.
A multi-type scoop takes the passing kind with the highest base weight (equal weights tie-break alphabetically by kind name). Order is now exec_move, launch / project_pain, new_hire, funding, hiring_plan, ma, then partnership / intent.

## Phase 2 dry-run fixes (branch phase2/fix-triggers)
First real dry run: 648 scoops, 258 kept, 20 ranked accounts with a trigger. Examples that scored but are not Pendo-buyer events, and what happens now:
- **Product launch of lab consumables:** the full 0.80 needs a digital-product word (app, platform, software, digital, mobile, portal, online, website, web, SaaS, AI, cloud, API, e-commerce, customer experience, CX/UX, dashboard, chatbot, login...). Otherwise the scoop is kept at 0.40 with the note 'physical or unclear product launch: check it is digital' (on the verdict reason and as an evidence suffix; `signal_notes(signal)` reads it back). Whole-word match: 'apple' is not 'app'.
- **'Pickles to be acquired by EQT' typed Funding:** a Funding scoop with target wording (to be acquired by, acquired by, has been bought by, taken over by, sold to, merger with, takeover bid from) and no money received (raised / secured / received / closed / Series / seed) is reclassified as M&A (reason 'Funding (reclassified as M&A: company is being acquired)'). Acquirer wording on a Funding scoop ('took a stake in') is NOT reclassified: it stays on the investor-note path and drops.
- **M&A direction (`ma_role`, `classify_ma`):** deterministic text rules around the scoop company's name (first word). Passive wording makes the company the TARGET unless it is named after the phrase (then it is the buyer). Active wording (to acquire, acquires, acquired, to buy, takes / took / taken a stake in, takeover / acquisition / buyout of or for) makes it the ACQUIRER if named before the verb, the TARGET if named only after it, else unclear. Company not named in the text: unclear.
  - Target: 0.50, reason 'being acquired: leadership/platform change likely'.
  - Acquirer: 0.25 when a minority stake (a percentage under 50, 'minority', or a stake with no majority wording), or a non-digital sector (energy, mining, oil, gas, property, resources, agriculture, construction, utilities, pharma, food, hotels, shipping, steel, coal, lithium, gold) with no digital word, or a stake in a non-digital business, reason 'acquirer of a minority stake or unrelated business'. Otherwise 0.50, reason 'acquirer of a digital business or a full acquisition' (digital words: software, digital, data, platform, cloud, technology, SaaS, app, analytics, AI, product, CX, fintech, online, e-commerce, mobile, cyber).
  - Unclear: 0.25, reason 'M&A direction unclear: check the deal'.
- **Partnership:** 0.30 with a product / digital / customer / app / platform / software / mobile / portal / online / e-commerce / website / UX / CX word, 0.20 otherwise. 'AI', 'data', 'cloud', 'infrastructure' alone do not count, so an AI data-centre capacity deal gets 0.20 and a note.
- **Same deal, two accounts:** kept M&A scoops of different companies in the same `filter_scoops` batch are the same deal when each names the other's company, or one names the other and they share most of their capitalised names and figures (9.9%, $20m). Both are kept; each verdict reason gets '; same deal as <other>' and the evidence gets the note (`ScoopVerdict.same_deal_as`). `combined_strength` counts one deal once inside an account (`dedupe_deals`: M&A signals with the same names and figures count once, the strongest wins; different deals still combine).
- **Exec moves:** a role holder who LEFT is a valid trigger (a new leader is coming): 'CTO left to join another org', 'Interim Chief Digital Officer leaving', 'Head of Product has resigned' are kept with 'role ... vacated, new leader likely'. A departure is vacated only if the text names no incoming leader (joins, appointed, named, hired, replaces, successor...). A bare CEO departure is still dropped (no buyer role). The 'former X' background rule is unchanged: 'a former CIO of a rival lender' is not a vacancy.
- **Incoming start date:** 'effective 1 December', 'will start on 15 October', 'starting December 1, 2026', 'from 2026-12-01' (needs a hint word such as joins, starts, effective, appointed in the same sentence). A past start date is the age basis and the signal's `source_date` (reason 'started N days ago'), and it can rescue a scoop published beyond the 90-day limit. A future start date counts as age 0 and `source_date` stays the publish date. A year-less date takes the publish year, or the next year when that would be more than 60 days before publication. Applies to Executive Move / New Hire only.
- **Evidence notes:** `Signal.evidence` may end with ` (note)` groups: role ambiguous, physical or unclear product launch, acquirer of a minority stake or unrelated business, M&A direction unclear, partnership without product words, same deal as X. `signal_notes(signal)` lists them; `is_ambiguous(signal)` is the role flag. The description is shortened so evidence plus notes stays within 280 characters.
- **Debt funding:** wording such as debt, loan, credit facility / line, notes, bonds, debenture, revolving, refinancing, borrowing, facility makes a Funding scoop 0.25 with 'debt facility, not an equity round', unless equity wording (equity, Series A-F, seed, venture, IPO, private placement) is also present. 'secured $ in funding' with no debt words stays 0.60. Debt funding hits the floor after day 27.
- **Fund / property launches:** after the digital check fails, fund / REIT / trust / portfolio / ETF / property / mortgage / insurance policy or product / loan product / home loan / term deposit / annuity wording makes a launch 0.20 (x0.25 of 0.80) with 'financial or property product launch, not a digital product'. A bank, insurer or super fund launching an app, platform, portal, online tool still counts as digital (the digital check runs first). Digital words now also include 'solution(s)', 'technology' and CamelCase product names ending in AI ('SignalAI'); 'a new solution targeting higher education' is full weight. Limit: 'cleaning solution' also reads as digital.
- **C-suite promotions:** 'promoted / moves / moved / elevated from X to (the role of) Y' judges the NEW role (Y) and the VACATED role (X) separately, never the rest of the sentence. If neither is a buyer role the scoop is dropped ('C-suite move from 'Chief Financial Officer' to 'CEO': neither the new nor the vacated role is a buyer role'). New role a buyer role: kept ('new role ...'); only the vacated role a buyer role (CTO to CEO): kept as a vacancy ('vacated role ..., new leader likely'). If both are buyer roles the new role is reported.
- **Rumoured deals:** reportedly, in talks, rumoured, understood to, said to, considering, exploring, eyeing, weighing, approach make the ACQUIRER half weight (0.25 for a full acquisition, 0.125 for a minority stake) with 'reported talks, not a confirmed deal'. The target keeps 0.50: the rumour discount is applied to the acquirer only, as asked; the target of a rumoured deal is just as uncertain, so this is a judgement call to revisit. Sellers ('to sell', 'divests') have no rule yet and read as unclear.
- **Real-data calibration (dry run):** of 118 M&A scoops about 46 acquirer, 51 target, 6 seller, 15 unclear; of 45 funding scoops 44 are recipients.
- **Known limits:** M&A role needs the company named in the text; ZoomInfo scoops attached to a parent or alias name will read 'unclear' (0.25). Deal-pair detection only compares scoops in the same batch, and needs shared names or figures. The sector word lists are plain regex and will miss unseen wording. Intent is unchanged.

### Age limits and floor
| Kind | Max age (days) |
|---|---|
| exec_move, new_hire | 90 |
| funding, product_launch, ma, project_pain | 45 |
| partnership, hiring_plan, intent | 30 |

`KIND_MAX_AGE_DAYS`. A kept scoop is exactly at the limit on its last day and dropped one day later ('Dropped: expired, 46 days old (limit 45 for funding)'). 
**Two different age settings, do not mix them up:**
- `KIND_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (this module) decides when a scoop is DROPPED by `filter_scoops` ('Dropped: expired...'). `config.signals.max_age_days` is used here only for a kind missing from that table (none today).
- `config.signals.max_age_days` (45) is only a FALLBACK now. The ranker's recency is `1 - age / signals.max_age_for(best signal's kind, config)`, floored at 0, so an exec move 60 days old (limit 90) still earns recency points. (Resolved in 1f-final; earlier notes said the ranker used the config value as its horizon.)
**Phase 2b: the scoop pull window must be 90 days for people-move scoops** (Executive Move, New Hire, Management Move), otherwise the 90-day limit is never used. That is the caller's job; this module only filters what it is given.
Minimum strength floor `MIN_STRENGTH_FLOOR = 0.10`: a signal weaker than that is dropped with the reason 'faded below floor (0.09 < 0.10, N days old)'. With the base weights every kind is still at or above 0.10 on its last allowed day (exec_move 0.225, new_hire 0.1625, funding 0.1359, partnership 0.1114, hiring 0.2043, ma 0.1132, launch 0.1811). The reduced variants hit the floor earlier: physical launch after day 42, minority / unclear M&A after day 27, partnership without product words after day 21, ambiguous roles late in the 90-day window. Those scoops show up as 'Dropped: faded below floor'.

`strength = round(base * 0.5 ** (age_days / half_life), 4)`. Constants: `KIND_BASE_WEIGHT`, `RECENCY_HALF_LIFE_DAYS` (21), `KIND_HALF_LIFE_DAYS` (exec_move, new_hire: 45).
Literal checks: exec_move at 21 days 0.6513, at 45 days 0.45; funding at 45 days 0.1359; product_launch at 10 days 0.5751.
An ambiguous role (below) multiplies the strength by `AMBIGUOUS_ROLE_FACTOR` = 0.7 (so exec_move 5 days old: 0.8333 clear, 0.5833 ambiguous).
Several signals on one account: `combined = 1 - (1 - best) * prod(1 - 0.5 * other)` (`COMBINE_DAMPING`), e.g. [0.5, 0.4] gives 0.6, [0.5, 0.4, 0.2] gives 0.64.

A multi-type scoop takes the passing kind with the highest base weight (so Executive Move + M&A is exec_move, Project + Partnership is project_pain). Equal weights (product_launch / project_pain, both 0.80) tie-break alphabetically by kind name. There is no separate precedence list.

## Role rules (Executive Move / New Hire / Management Move)
`role_match(desc) -> RoleMatch(role, ambiguous)` (`relevant_role(desc)` returns just the role).
- **Match:** a title word (chief, head, vp, director, manager, lead, owner...) next to a domain word (product, digital, customer experience / success / support / service / care, CX, UX, engineering, technology, IT, mobile, digital workplace, employee experience, transformation), with at most ONE free word between them ("Head of Platform Engineering"), or CTO / CPO / CDO / CIO / CXO, "Chief Customer Officer", "Chief Information and Digital/Technology Officer". The connector `for` does not link a title to a domain, and `officer` is not a title word by itself.
- **Someone else's role:** "Executive Assistant to the CTO", "PA to the Head of Product" do not count (lookbehind for assistant / PA / EA / secretary / chief of staff + to/for).
- **Non-buyer function:** rejected when the role phrase or up to 3 words after it contain supply, compliance, procurement, marketing, social media, manufacturing, finance, legal, facilities, safety, sales, risk, warehouse, aged care, hospital, network, support services, HR, people, recruiting. The window stops at to / who / will / as / after / that / which / previously / formerly / from / at, so "Chief Customer Officer to unify sales, success and support" is kept.
- **Physical-goods product roles:** a `product` role followed (within 4 words) by food, dairy, wine, beverage, plant, factory, FMCG, grocery, produce, supermarket, apparel, fresh, meat, bakery, pharma, mining, merchandise, agriculture, farm, textile, quality assurance, assurance is dropped ("Product Manager, Dairy", "Head of Product Quality Assurance for its food plant").
- **Ambiguous (kept, flagged):** a `product` role where either the rest of the description has retail / store / manufacturing / food / equipment / pharma / construction context, or the role is followed by a comma and a category word that is not a digital word or geography ("Head of Product, Footwear", "Product Lead, Home Loans"). The verdict reason ends 'role ambiguous: check it is a digital product role' and the strength is x0.7. Regex cannot settle these; the call sheet shows the reason so Alex can judge.
- `_MARKETING` pre-filter removed: `marketing` in the negative list already drops "Product Marketing Manager" and "Digital Marketing Lead".
Role table: `tests/fixtures/signals_role_table.json` (the reviewer's 85 invented rows): 41 TP, 0 FP, 0 FN, 44 TN. Rows 76, 77 and 85 (a nurse / paralegal / medical hire with "digital", "app" or "product launch" in the text) were relabelled from keep to drop (decision H2).

## Intent (UNVERIFIED)
`intent_to_signals(rows, accounts, config, today, skipped=None)`. The row shape is our guess and has not been checked against the live ZoomInfo `search_intent` connector:
`{company_id, topic, signal_score 60-100, audience_strength A-E, recorded_date?}`. Never called live.
- Kind `SignalKind.INTENT`, strength = (score - 60) / 40 x audience factor (A 1.0, B 0.85, C 0.7, D 0.55, E 0.4), capped at 0.3 while UNVERIFIED, then decayed with a 21-day half-life.
- A row with no `recorded_date` is treated as 21 days old, never as brand new (so undated 100/A = 0.15). Older than 30 days is skipped ('expired').
- A score of exactly 60 gives strength 0, so the row is SKIPPED with a reason (not floored). Anything below the 0.10 floor is skipped as 'faded below floor'.
- Evidence reads 'ZoomInfo intent (UNVERIFIED shape): interest signal, not a dated event. ...' so the call sheet can label it honestly.
- `best_signal` never returns an intent signal while the account has any real (non-intent) signal; `combined_strength` then counts intent only as a damped extra (x0.5). Example: real 0.16 + intent 0.3 = 0.286. Intent-only accounts are labelled by their evidence.
- Topics: digital experience, product analytics, customer experience, digital adoption, product onboarding, user onboarding. "Onboarding" alone is not accepted.
- `Signal.source` = `zoominfo_intent:<company_id>:<topic>`.
- A 0.3 cap on its own would not stop intent beating a decayed scoop (a 45-day-old funding scoop is 0.16), which is why `best_signal` / `combined_strength` rank real signals first. Intent is not part of `trigger_radar`.

## Round 4 fixes (branch phase1/1e-fixes)
- **Shared ZoomInfo ids:** `scoops_to_signals` takes `Mapping[str, Account | list[Account]]`. Every account in a list gets its own copy of the signal (same kind, strength, source, date, evidence; only `sf_account_id` differs). `trigger_radar` and `intent_to_signals` do the same (earlier: first account only). An empty list counts as unmatched.
- **Ambiguity travels with the signal:** a kept ambiguous role gets the suffix ` (role ambiguous: check it is a digital product role)` on `Signal.evidence`; `signals.is_ambiguous(signal)` tests for it. The suffix always fits inside `EVIDENCE_MAX_CHARS` (the description is shortened). The shared `Signal` model has no flag field, so this is the minimal carrier; a real field would be cleaner.
- **Investor notes (general):** investor wording now covers invest(ed/ment), participated in, led/co-led a round / investment / Series / seed, took/taken/acquired a stake in, backed/backing. If the company comes before the verb in the same clause (no 'raised/secured/received' between) it is the investor: dropped. If it comes after ('Fund Y led a round in <company>') it is the investee: kept. Otherwise kept only if money received is mentioned (raised, secured, received, closed, funding, Series X, seed). 'Backed by' alone is not a funding event; 'raised $5M, backed by Fund Z' is. 'Round' and 'backed by' no longer count as money received.
- **Role context:** a role preceded within 2 filler words by former / formerly / previously / ex-, or right after replaces / replacing / succeeds (optionally 'the former'), is not the new leader; 'She succeeds Jane as Chief Digital Officer' still counts. Any description that mentions board, chair or non-executive rejects every role in it ('The Board welcomed a new Chief Digital Officer' is dropped: a known false negative).
- **CIO:** the acronym is rejected when its sentence mentions investment, portfolio, funds / assets under management, assets, superannuation or AUM (Chief Investment Officer). The spelled-out 'Chief Information Officer' is unaffected.
- **Hiring path:** the non-buyer word list applies to hiring-role phrases, and bare `product` is a weak keyword ('Product Compliance Officer', 'Product Safety Auditor' drop; 'Product Manager / Owner / Designer / Lead / Team' still count).
- **Chief X and Y Officer:** parsed generally; counts when X or Y is a buyer domain word or 'information' ('Chief Customer and Digital Officer', 'Chief Product and Growth Officer'); 'Chief Risk and Compliance Officer' does not. Hyphenated free words are allowed ('Head of E-commerce Product').

## Second dry run fixes (branch phase2/fix-triggers2)
- **Stacking (combined_strength), as revised after review:** `combined = 1 - (1 - best) * prod(1 - contribution)`. Steps: (1) same-deal M&A and near-identical scoops count once; (2) pick the best signal of each KIND first: the strongest counts in full, the best of every other kind counts at 0.5 (`COMBINE_DAMPING`); (3) further signals of a kind that already has a best add at most 0.15 each (`SAME_KIND_EXTRA_EACH`), 0.30 together (`SAME_KIND_EXTRA_TOTAL`); (4) only the `MAX_COMBINED_SIGNALS` = 4 strongest counted entries (the best plus up to three others) are used. Kind diversity is decided before the cap, so FUNDING 0.50 / 0.49 / 0.48 plus NEW_HIRE 0.45 gives 0.72 (the hire counts at 0.225), not 0.6388. Note: the brief said 'overall cap of 3' and 'about 0.72'; counting the best plus three others reproduces 0.72 and keeps the nine-launch Catapult case at 0.7038 (the 0.30 extras total binds there). Intent still cannot be best. One signal per kind is unchanged (0.5 + 0.4 = 0.6).
- **Near-identical scoops (`dedupe_signals`):** same kind, and either the same first 60 normalised characters of the evidence (lower case, punctuation and spacing ignored, notes stripped) or the same product name (the capitalised name after launch / unveil / introduce / release / debut / roll out / announce) within 14 days (`NEAR_DUPLICATE_CHARS`, `NEAR_DUPLICATE_DAYS`). The strongest is kept. This runs inside `combined_strength`; the signals themselves are all still returned by `scoops_to_signals`. A product name with a lower-case suffix ('PerformanceHub 2') is matched on its capitalised part.
- **M&A, third-party construction:** 'NEXTDC has entered into an agreement for Goodman and Digital Realty to acquire a 9.9% stake in the company': the buyers are the words between 'agreement / deal / offer / proposal / bid / allow(s) / enable(s) / permit(s) (for)' and 'to acquire'. A company named inside that span is the acquirer; a company named before it, or after the verb, is the TARGET. (Before this fix NEXTDC read as the acquirer.)
- **Minority stake purchase of the company:** a TARGET whose text describes a minority stake (a percentage under 50 with 'stake', 'minority', or a stake with no majority wording) is 0.35 (`MA_TARGET_MINORITY_WEIGHT`), reason 'minority stake purchase: weak trigger', because a 9.9% holder is not a leadership or platform change. Majority, full acquisition, 'to be acquired by' targets stay 0.50. At 5 days: NEXTDC 0.2967 (0.4239 x 0.7), Goodman 0.2119.
- **Launches: hardware beats weak digital words.** If the text has a hardware word (robotics, robot, instrument, consumable, sampling, emitter, camera, sensor, device, hardware, laboratory, chromatography, equipment, powder, reagent, assay, spectrometer, analyser, wearable, drone, battery, microscope, centrifuge, pipette) the launch is non-digital (0.40) unless the text names a software product itself (software, app, SaaS, portal, website, API, e-commerce, banking app, chatbot, web app). 'platform', 'user interface', 'AI', 'cloud', 'mobile', 'digital', 'online' do not rescue it. 'A scaled-up robotics platform with a new user interface' is hardware; 'a camera with a new mobile app' stays digital. Software launches with no hardware word are unchanged.
- **Test needing a shared change:** `tests/test_integration_pipeline.py::test_m_ranker_uses_the_single_signals_combination_formula` hard-codes the old formula (two same-kind funding signals 0.5 and 0.4 = 0.6, and identical evidence 'e'). It must use different kinds and distinct evidence (0.5 funding + 0.4 M&A = 0.6; add a 0.2 launch = 0.64). Not edited here (not an owned file).

(3) Place and generic words (Australia, New Zealand, Sydney, Asia Pacific, expansion, range, version...) are not product names: 'launches New Zealand expansion of lending app' and 'launched New Zealand credit card' no longer dedupe by name. (4) KNOWN LIMIT: a lower-case product name ('launches the pro tracker') cannot be matched by name; only the first-60-characters rule applies. (5) application(s), webapp(s), online store / shop count as software nouns and digital words, so 'launches an application for battery monitoring' and 'Powder Coat Pro online store' stay digital.
- **Exact note strings (final review):** `signal_notes` now matches the full note texts (`KNOWN_NOTES`), not prefixes, so a scoop ending in '(being acquired by Bigco)' is plain evidence. 'same deal as <names>' is the one dynamic note (that exact prefix plus at least one name). The old `NOTE_PREFIXES` constant is removed. `callsheet.NOTE_BANNED_PHRASES` gained 'minority stake purchase' (takeover, being acquired, acquisition of the company, full acquisition, acquired by, buyout) and 'role vacated' (has appointed, appointed a new, successor, replacement, has hired, new head, new chief, congrats on the new role, congratulations on the new role, welcome the new); phrases the scoop text itself contains are left out as before.

## Round 3 M&A direction fixes (branch phase2/fix-triggers3)
- **'for' buyer span:** the buyer span now follows ANY 'for' ('a scheme of arrangement for Bigco to acquire Acme', 'approval from FIRB for Bigco to acquire Acme', 'agreement for X to acquire') as well as allow / enable / permit. A 'for' span counts only when it names a company (a capitalised word), so 'approval for the deal to acquire Zeta' and 'approval for it to acquire Zeta' keep the company as the acquirer. 'agreement with Bigco to acquire Zeta' and 'approval from the ACCC to acquire Zeta' have no 'for' and stay acquirer cases.
- **Percentage without 'stake':** 'Bigco to acquire 30% of Acme' and 'Bigco acquires 19.9% of Acme' make Acme a minority-stake target (0.35, 'minority stake purchase: weak trigger'). 50% or more, 'all', 'full', 'remaining', 'majority', 'controlling' stay 0.50 targets ('the remaining 49% of Acme' is a full acquisition). Only the pattern '<n>% of' counts; growth figures ('up 30% in revenue') do not.
